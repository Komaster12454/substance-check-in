    Why there is no route-equivalence calculator

    Swallowed vs inhaled vs sublingual is not just a unit conversion. Bioavailability, onset, peak concentration, metabolism, formulation, and technique can all change the experience. Turning those into “take X by route B” would be falsely precise and bad harm reduction.

    Derivative ≠ flavor

    LSD analogues, 2C-x compounds, cannabinoids, dissociatives, and research chemicals can differ substantially. A shared family name does not make potency or duration interchangeable.

    Unknown still means unknown

    A colorful package, blotter art, powder color, pill shape, “mushroom” branding, or seller description cannot establish identity. The less certain the identity, the less useful any effect chart becomes.
